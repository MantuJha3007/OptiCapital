# AEGIS Institutional Investment Policy Statement (IPS)

## Mandate & Portfolio Objectives
The Fund operates under an institutional multi-asset capital preservation mandate. The primary fiduciary objective is the long-term compounding of capital while strictly bounding peak-to-trough drawdowns within the Safe Operating Envelope.

## Safe Operating Envelope (SOE) Policy
The Fund recognizes four distinct operational risk regimes based on the composite AEGIS Risk Score (0–100):

### 1. GREEN (Safe / Normal Operation): Score 0.0 – 29.9
- Permissible Equity Exposure: Up to 50.0%.
- Minimum Cash Reserve: 5.0%.
- Maximum Annualized Volatility: 15.0%.
- Action: Normal surveillance. Unnecessary portfolio turnover is explicitly prohibited.

### 2. YELLOW (Caution / Elevated Risk): Score 30.0 – 59.9
- Permissible Equity Exposure: Up to 45.0%.
- Minimum Cash Reserve: 10.0%.
- Maximum Annualized Volatility: 14.0%.
- Action: Tighten risk budgets; issue advisory alert to the Risk Officer.

### 3. ORANGE (High Risk / Boundary Breach): Score 60.0 – 79.9
- Permissible Equity Exposure: Up to 35.0%.
- Minimum Cash Reserve: 15.0%.
- Maximum Annualized Volatility: 12.0%.
- Action: Generate candidate defensive rebalance allocation.

### 4. RED (Crisis / Mandatory Control): Score 80.0 – 100.0
- Permissible Equity Exposure: Strictly capped at 20.0% - 30.0%.
- Minimum Cash Reserve: Elevated to 15.0% - 20.0%.
- Maximum Annualized Volatility: Capped at 10.0%.
- Action: Mandatory Minimum-Intervention rebalance. The Risk Officer must review and execute defensive de-risking.

## Hysteresis & Anti-Churning Clause
To prevent destructive portfolio churning caused by transient noise near threshold boundaries, AEGIS mandates a 3.0-point hysteresis recovery band:
- Recovery from RED to ORANGE requires a verified score drop to <= 77.0.
- Recovery from ORANGE to YELLOW requires a verified score drop to <= 57.0.
- Recovery from YELLOW to GREEN requires a verified score drop to <= 27.0.
